Privacy Policy for Nihongo MM

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Nihongo MM is a Japanese language learning application designed for Burmese-speaking learners.

Information We Collect

Nihongo MM does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Local Data Storage

Nihongo MM stores learning-related information locally on your device to provide app functionality. This may include:

  • Vocabulary study progress
  • Favorites
  • SRS and review progress
  • Quiz progress and accuracy
  • Study streak and activity
  • Daily learning goals and preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Nihongo MM servers.

Removing the app may remove locally stored app data depending on your device and backup settings.

Text-to-Speech

Nihongo MM uses your device's text-to-speech functionality to pronounce Japanese words.

The app does not use the microphone for this feature and does not record your voice.

Text-to-speech processing is provided by the text-to-speech service installed or configured on your device and may be subject to that service provider's own privacy practices.

Data Sharing

Nihongo MM does not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

Accounts

Nihongo MM currently does not provide user accounts and does not require registration or login.

Children

Nihongo MM is an educational language-learning application. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Nihongo MM's features or data practices change. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
